# The One disk

![the-one-disk](the-one-disk.png)

A parody of the One Ring from J.R.R. Tolkien's works: a 3.5" hard drive
being forged on an anvil in the fires of Orodruin (Mount Doom).
The glowing red casing bears the Ring's Elvish inscription on its edges.

The image is derived from [the-one-1024x1024](the-one-1024x1024.jpg).
